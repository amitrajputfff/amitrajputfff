### Hi, I'm Amit. I'm building [Align](https://meet-align.com).

Every standup ends with *"I'll update the ticket."* Nobody does, and by Thursday the board and reality are two different things.

Align does that work for you. It listens to the call, catches who committed to what, and turns it into tickets with an owner and a due date, synced both ways with **Jira** or **Linear**. Open a PR and the ticket moves to review; merge it and it's done. Tomorrow's standup writes itself from what actually shipped.

**Try it in 30 seconds, no signup:** type your standup update at **[meet-align.com](https://meet-align.com)** and see the tickets it makes.
